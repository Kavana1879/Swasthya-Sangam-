# 🏥 Swasthya Sangam
## Holistic Medication Interaction and Safety Assistant

> Check Before You Take. Protect Every Dose.

Swasthya Sangam is an AI-powered healthcare application that helps users check whether medicines(Allopathic), Ayurvedic products, herbal supplements, and nutritional supplements are safe to take together.

Many people combine prescription medicines with traditional remedies and dietary supplements without knowing that some combinations can cause serious side effects such as internal bleeding, low blood sugar, or reduced absorption. Swasthya Sangam was created to provide a simple and understandable safety check before taking multiple products together.

The application allows users to select up to six medicines or supplements at one time. It analyzes all selected combinations and provides a clear result using three color-coded risk levels: Safe, Caution, and Dangerous. The system also explains why an interaction may occur, identifies the active ingredients involved, suggests better timing, and recommends safer alternatives when possible.

## 🚦 Risk Levels

### 🟢 Safe
No major interaction was identified in the current database.

### 🟡 Caution
A potential interaction exists. Timing adjustments or monitoring may be helpful.

### 🔴 Dangerous
A serious interaction may be present. Professional medical advice is strongly recommended.

## 🧪 Example Interactions

### Dangerous
- Warfarin + Aspirin → Severe bleeding risk
- Warfarin + Ibuprofen → Internal bleeding
- Clopidogrel + Aspirin → Very high bleeding risk
- Aspirin + Diclofenac → Stomach bleeding risk

### Caution
- Iron + Calcium → Reduced absorption
- Metformin + Fenugreek → Low blood sugar risk
- Amlodipine + Ashwagandha → Low blood pressure and dizziness

### Safe
- Vitamin D3 + Calcium → Supports bone health
- Iron + Vitamin C → Improves absorption
- Omega-3 + Vitamin D3 → Supports heart health

## ⏱️ Time-Based Intelligence

The application does more than detect interactions. It also suggests better timing.

Examples:
- Iron at night and Calcium in the morning
- Warfarin in the morning and Ashwagandha at night
- Metformin after breakfast and Fenugreek in the evening

These suggestions help users reduce the chance of interaction and improve absorption.

## 📚 Real-World Motivation

A widely recognized medication safety concern is the combination of Warfarin and Aspirin. Both products affect blood clotting, and when taken together without proper medical supervision, they can significantly increase the risk of severe internal bleeding.

This type of interaction inspired us to create Swasthya Sangam, a simple tool that helps users check combinations before taking them.
